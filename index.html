Grandma's Lasagna

A rich, cheesy baked lasagna layered with meat sauce, ricotta, and mozzarella — a family favorite passed down for generations.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 box lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Brown the ground beef in a pan and mix in the marinara sauce.
  3. Layer noodles, ricotta, meat sauce, and mozzarella in a baking dish, repeating until ingredients are used up.
  4. Top with parmesan and cover with foil.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes, then remove foil and bake 10 more minutes until golden.
  6. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
